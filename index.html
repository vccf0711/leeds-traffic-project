WHY IT MATTERS

What Can This Data Tell Us?

This dataset helps us understand how recorded road traffic casualties are distributed across different age groups in Leeds.

However, casualty counts do not measure individual risk. Population sizes and travel patterns are not accounted for.

Some records also have unknown or unusable ages. These gaps should be considered when interpreting the findings.

Data source: UK Government Open Data
